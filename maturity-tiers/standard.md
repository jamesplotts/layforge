Typical tabletop-adventure content: combat violence, peril, and morally
complex situations are all fine, narrated with normal genre-appropriate
detail — wounds, danger, and consequences can be real without being
gratuitously graphic. Mild profanity in dialogue is fine. Romantic
content can be implied or referenced but should fade to black rather
than being described explicitly. No sexual content.

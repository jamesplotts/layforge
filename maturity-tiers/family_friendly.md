Keep all content appropriate for a general audience, including children.
Avoid graphic descriptions of violence, injury, or death — combat
outcomes can still carry real stakes and impact without gore. No
profanity. Romantic content stays at most a G-rated hand-hold or a
chaste kiss mentioned only in passing, never dwelt on. Horror elements
should stay at a "spooky adventure story" level — tense and exciting,
not genuinely frightening or disturbing.

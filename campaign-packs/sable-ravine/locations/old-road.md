# The Old Road

A day's walk of rutted cart-track between Stonewatch and the ravine,
running through increasingly unkempt scrub as the keep's patrol range
falls behind. Old boundary cairns, half-collapsed, still mark where
farmland gave up and wilderness took over — nobody's maintained them in
years.

Wagon-wheel ruts and hoofprints are common near the keep end and thin out
entirely past the halfway cairn; whatever's been raiding supply wagons
doesn't seem to be following the road the whole way, which is worth a
party noticing if they think to look for tracks (`resolve_check`,
Wisdom, DC 12 — SRD's default "moderate" difficulty is the right call
here, nothing about this trail is trying to hide itself from a careful
observer).

See `encounters/ambush-on-the-old-road.md` for what actually happens if
the party is followed rather than following.

# The Goblin Camp

A cluster of hide tents and lashed-together lean-tos on a wide ledge,
ringed with a crude palisade of sharpened stakes salvaged from the very
wagons they've been raiding. Cookfire smoke is visible from the ravine
floor most evenings — this band isn't hiding, it's confident, which is
new.

Roughly two dozen goblins live here under Chief Skreel (see
`npcs/goblin-chief-skreel.md`), along with whatever captives and loot
haven't yet been moved along. A supply-wagon's worth of crates, some
still intact, sit under a tarp near the chief's own tent — evidence
enough, if the party finds it, that this camp is behind the road raids
specifically, not just wilderness banditry in general.

Two of Stonewatch's missing scouts are held here as captives, bound but
alive — Skreel has been keeping them rather than killing them, on
instruction he doesn't fully understand and won't explain unless pressed
hard (see `npcs/goblin-chief-skreel.md`'s personality notes on what it
takes to get that out of him).

This camp is a legitimate fight if the party comes in loud, and a
legitimate negotiation if they don't — Skreel is proud, not suicidal, and
the DM should play him as genuinely willing to talk before it comes to
blows, not as a boss monster waiting to be triggered.

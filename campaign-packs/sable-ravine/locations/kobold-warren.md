# The Kobold Warren

A low, well-hidden tunnel mouth in the ravine's western wall, easy to
walk past entirely without a deliberate search
(`resolve_check`, Wisdom, DC 14). Inside, a warren of narrow,
low-ceilinged passages home to a wary, practical kobold community under
First Digger Nix (see `npcs/first-digger-nix.md`) — a few dozen kobolds,
none of them eager for a fight they'd lose.

The warren is trap-happy near its entrance (deadfalls, noise-snares
strung with bones and shale) and genuinely undefended deeper in, where
the kobolds actually live and work. They are not responsible for the
road raids and would very much like the party to know that before anyone
starts swinging — Nix would rather trade information for safe passage
than fight anyone, human or goblin.

The kobolds know things worth knowing: that the goblins have gotten
"bold and stupid" lately, that something with "a cold voice" lives at
the ravine's dead end and has been talked about in whispers even the
kobolds don't fully understand, and that they themselves have started
leaving small offerings at the ravine's dead end out of simple fear —
not worship, just not wanting to be next. This is the party's clearest
lead toward `ruined-shrine.md` if the goblin camp doesn't give it to them
first.

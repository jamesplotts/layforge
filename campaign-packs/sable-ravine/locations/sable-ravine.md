# The Sable Ravine

A mile-long gash in black shale, steep enough in places to need real
climbing, its floor a maze of scree and switchback trail. Three things
worth finding live here, and the ravine's own geography keeps them from
noticing each other's business day to day:

- **The goblin camp** (`goblin-camp`), staked out on a wide ledge partway
  down the eastern wall — see that file. This is who's actually been
  hitting the road.
- **The kobold warren** (`kobold-warren`), tunneled into the ravine floor
  near its western end — see that file. Not the source of the raids, but
  not uninvolved either.
- **The ruined shrine** (`ruined-shrine`), at the ravine's dead end where
  the walls close in and the shale gives way to older, dressed stone —
  see that file, and `npcs/the-hollow-flame.md`. The actual reason the
  raids sharpened.

A party entering the ravine without a specific destination in mind
should get a general sense of scale and unease before anything finds
them — this isn't a single room, it's a landscape, and the DM should
feel free to let travel between the ravine's locations take real
narrated time rather than treating them as adjacent doors.

# The Ruined Shrine

Where the Sable Ravine dead-ends, the black shale gives way abruptly to
older, pale, dressed stone — the remains of a shrine far older than
Stonewatch, its purpose and original occupant long forgotten by everyone
except, apparently, the thing that still lives there. Broken columns,
a collapsed roof open to the sky, and at the center, a cracked altar
still faintly warm to the touch for reasons nobody who's touched it has
lived to explain.

This is where `npcs/the-hollow-flame.md` actually resides, and the true
source of the ravine's sudden troubles — not a mastermind pulling
strings so much as a slow-burning influence, unsettling the goblins into
overconfidence and the kobolds into superstitious dread just by existing
nearby, amplified by whatever small offerings and attention it's been
getting from both.

Physically guarding the shrine's inner sanctum is a single animated
guardian (see `encounters/shrine-guardian.md`) — not a horde, a
gatekeeper. The Hollow Flame itself does not fight in any conventional
sense; see that NPC file for how the DM should actually run the
confrontation.

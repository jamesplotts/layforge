# Stonewatch

A squat, weather-scoured keep of grey stone, built two generations ago to
watch a border road nobody important travels anymore. Its garrison is
twenty soldiers where a hundred were budgeted for; half the outer wall's
arrow-loops have been quietly bricked up rather than staffed.

- **The Gatehouse** — a single guard on a normal day, two since the
  raids sharpened. Anyone arriving after dark is made to wait for a
  lantern-check before the gate opens, no exceptions, no matter how
  urgent they claim it is.
- **The Common Hall** — doubles as mess, tavern, and the only warm room
  in the keep. This is where rumors actually circulate: a trapper's
  wild story about lights in the ravine at night, a supply clerk
  grumbling about a wagon that arrived three days late and one crate
  short, a soldier's superstition about not whistling on the old road
  after dusk.
- **The Garrison Yard** — Captain Vashti can usually be found here
  drilling the handful of soldiers she has left, or arguing with the
  quartermaster about rations. See `npcs/captain-orlen-vashti.md`.

Stonewatch has no cleric of its own and no healer beyond a competent
army surgeon — a party expecting to rest and fully recover here should
be told plainly that beds and a roof are on offer, not miracles.

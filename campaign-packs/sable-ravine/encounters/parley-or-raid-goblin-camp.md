# Parley or Raid: The Goblin Camp

Not a single fixed encounter — this is the camp's actual decision point,
and the DM should let the party's approach genuinely determine which
version happens.

**If the party comes in loud** (announced, weapons out, or already
spotted and alerted per `ambush-on-the-old-road.md`): the camp musters to
defend itself. Roughly two dozen goblins live here, but not all of them
fight at once or well — treat it as waves rather than one mass melee,
and let Skreel try to rally a defense before deciding whether to fight
personally or flee deeper into the ravine if it's clearly going badly for
him. He is proud, not suicidal.

**If the party approaches openly and signals they want to talk**: Skreel
will parley, flanked by a few guards, posturing hard but genuinely
willing to negotiate — see `npcs/goblin-chief-skreel.md` for how to play
him. A successful Charisma-based `resolve_check` (DC 13–15 depending on
how the party's actually playing the conversation, DM's call) can get
him talking about "the voice from the old stones," get the captives
released without a fight, or both.

**Either way**, the two captive Stonewatch scouts (alive, bound, held
near Skreel's tent) are the clearest proof this camp is behind the road
raids, and the clearest emotional stakes if the party has any connection
to Stonewatch. Freeing them — by force or by negotiation — should feel
like a real accomplishment regardless of which path got them there.

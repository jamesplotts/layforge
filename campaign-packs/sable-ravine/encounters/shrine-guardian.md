# The Shrine Guardian

The Sable Ravine's actual climactic fight. Bound to the cracked altar at
the ruined shrine's heart is a single animated undead guardian — statted
as a real SRD creature (an SRD Ghast fits the tone: fast, vicious, and a
believable "last line of defense" without being an unbeatable set-piece
boss for a level 1–3 party; a DM running a tougher or gentler table
should feel free to reskin the same encounter with a weaker or stronger
SRD undead instead).

The guardian does not negotiate and does not flee — it fights until
destroyed, full stop, and shouldn't be talked down the way Skreel can be.
It's the one purely mechanical fight in this pack; resolve it with
`resolve_check`/`apply_effect` normally, no special narrative gating.

Unlike the guardian, **the Hollow Flame itself is not part of this
fight** — see `npcs/the-hollow-flame.md` for why that NPC should be run
as an ongoing presence/conversation rather than something this combat
encounter defeats directly. A party that destroys the guardian has won
the physical fight and earned the right to actually confront (or
bargain with, or simply walk away from) whatever's still whispering from
the altar — that's the campaign's actual ending, and it's deliberately
not scripted here beyond what `the-hollow-flame.md` already says about
what meaningfully reduces its influence.

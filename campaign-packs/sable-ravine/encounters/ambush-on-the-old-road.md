# Ambush on the Old Road

Triggers once, roughly halfway along the old road, if the party is
traveling toward the ravine without having already made contact with
the goblins some other way. A scouting party of four to six goblins
(SRD Goblin) has been shadowing anyone heading up the road since the
raids sharpened — Skreel wants warning if Stonewatch sends anyone
serious.

They don't attack immediately. First, a `resolve_check` (Wisdom, DC 13)
for anyone keeping watch to notice movement in the scrub before the
goblins are ready — success means the party gets the first move instead
of the ambush landing clean. If nobody's watching, the goblins get one
free hit each on a surprised target before combat formally starts
(`start_combat`), same as any other unnoticed ambush.

The goblins will break and flee back toward the camp once they've lost
half their number or their apparent leader — they're scouts, not
fanatics, and Skreel would rather get a warning back to camp than lose
his whole watch patrol proving a point. A fleeing goblin who escapes
means the goblin camp knows the party is coming; treat that as a real
consequence, not a wasted encounter — Skreel's camp should be visibly
more alert if the party arrives after a scout got away.

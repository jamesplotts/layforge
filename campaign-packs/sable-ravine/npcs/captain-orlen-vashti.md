Vashti has commanded Stonewatch's garrison for six years and has spent
most of them making do with less than she asked for. She is not a
politician and doesn't pretend to be one — if a report from the regional
lord's office comes up, expect a flat, unimpressed comment about people
who've never stood a night watch making decisions about how many people
should be standing night watch.

She hires the party because she's out of better options, not because
she's given up: she still expects results, still wants a full accounting
of what they find, and will be genuinely angry (not performatively — she
liked those scouts) if it turns out the missing scouts could have been
saved and weren't. She does not expect the party to solve the ravine's
"monster problem" permanently, and says so if asked — she'd settle for
the raids stopping and the road being safe again.

Pay: what she can actually spare, stated honestly up front rather than
oversold — this is a cash-poor garrison, and Vashti won't promise more
than she can deliver.

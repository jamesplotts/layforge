Nix leads the warren by consensus more than authority — kobolds who
disagree with a First Digger's decisions tend to just quietly stop
digging where told, so Nix has learned to lead by being reliably useful
rather than by being obeyed. Nix wants exactly one thing from the party:
for this to end without the warren getting caught in the middle of
whatever's coming.

Genuinely willing to trade information for safety, and doesn't consider
this cowardice — Nix will say, plainly, that the warren survives by not
being anyone's fight. Knows real, useful things (see
`locations/kobold-warren.md`) and will offer them unprompted if the
party doesn't come in swinging. Will not fight to protect the goblins or
anything at the ruined shrine, and says so if asked whose side the
kobolds are on: nobody's, on purpose.

If the party later tells Skreel that the kobolds talked, Nix finds out —
`shared_knowledge: strict` means the DM should actually let that
consequence land (a cooled reception on a return visit, kobolds posting
their own watchers, etc.), not ignore it because it's inconvenient.

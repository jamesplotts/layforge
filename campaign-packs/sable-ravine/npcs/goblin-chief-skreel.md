Skreel didn't used to be this confident, and doesn't fully understand
why he is now — three months ago he'd have run a raid or two a season and
called it a good year; now he's hitting supply wagons on a schedule and
has started calling himself something grander than "chief" when he
thinks the tribe's listening. He genuinely believes the run of luck is
his own doing. It isn't (see `npcs/the-hollow-flame.md`).

Skreel is proud and easily wounded in his pride, but not stupid — he
will posture, threaten, and demand tribute before he commits his people
to a fight he might lose, and can be talked out of violence by anyone
willing to flatter him a little and offer him a face-saving way out. He
does not want to explain the missing-scouts situation, because he
doesn't have a good answer: he's been keeping them because "the voice
from the old stones" told him captives were more useful alive than dead,
and he's uneasy admitting out loud that he's taking orders from
something in the ruined shrine.

Pressed hard (a good Charisma-based `resolve_check`, or simply
cornering him with no other option) he'll admit that much, at least — not
because he's brave, but because at some level he wants someone else to
go deal with the voice so he doesn't have to.
